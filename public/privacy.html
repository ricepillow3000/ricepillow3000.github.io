WHAT THIS SITE COLLECTS

Nothing about you is collected, sold or shared by me. There are no accounts, no forms, no ads, no cookies and no analytics or tracking scripts.

  • Saved on your device only. Your game progress, save files and settings (sound, volume, reduced motion) are kept in your own browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data or using CLEAR DATA in the game removes them.
  • Fonts. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them and Google receives your IP address as part of that request. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Weather. The calendar shows Atlanta's weather from the free Open-Meteo service. The request is for Atlanta's fixed location, never yours, and is sent with no cookies or referrer.
  • Hosting. The site is served by Vercel (and mirrored on GitHub Pages). Like any web host, they may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, to run and protect their service.
  • Links out. LinkedIn, GitHub, the live project apps and your email app are separate services with their own privacy policies.
